# Component patterns

Use the component's information hierarchy to choose a variant. Measurements are CSS pixels at the observed reference widths. The screen abbreviations link back to [analysis evidence](ANALYSIS_EVIDENCE.md) and exact values are available in the [token file](DESIGN_TOKENS.json).

## Shells, cards and inset surfaces

**White anchor.** Use white with 28–32 px rounding for the principal object. Pair it with the low shadow over lavender when the edge needs definition. Reference pairings include 32 px radius with 24 px padding for the desktop ticket, 30 px with 20 px for the confirmation ticket, and 28 px with 16 px for the calendar date card. In the calendar, the sticky roster and work area are parts of a single white board.

**Flat task panel.** Use one pale fill to bind a set of related controls, a summary or a short content module. Desktop specialist and time cards use 28 px corners and 20 px padding. Mobile uses 26 px and 16 px. The mobile bundle and service list use 24 px corners. Shape and color carry most of the boundary.

**Inset.** Step down the radius and shift the surface tone. A peach date/time inset uses 22 px radius and 16 px padding in the receipt and BR10 details. A compact numeric strip in the mobile hero has a 16 px radius. Divide adjacent values with a 1 px line at low ink alpha. Use one level of nesting when the inner cluster has a clear purpose and let text or separators handle the rest.

**Review or supporting quote.** A shallow pastel panel has a star row and timestamp at the top, a dark semibold quote, then reviewer portrait and metadata. P1 uses lilac, 28 px radius and 20 px padding. P2 uses peach, 26 px radius and 16 px padding, with a simpler identity row. It follows the ticket on desktop and the time picker on mobile. Keep supporting social proof visually below the active choice.

## Navigation and utilities

**Desktop navigation.** Put the brand on the leading edge, the destinations in a white capsule and utilities at the trailing edge. P1 uses a 48 px-high capsule with 6 px padding and 4 px item gaps. Items are 36 px high, with 20 px horizontal padding and 14/20 at 600. The active item is ink/white and muted inactive items remain transparent. The visual center of the capsule can shift with unequal side groups. A separate breadcrumb row carries current location and local actions. It uses an inky final destination, low-contrast ancestors and compact chevrons.

**Mobile headers.** Keep the leading back or close control and trailing actions at the same optical height. Petal uses a black rounded-square tile with a small four-petal mark near the center of a short header. Confirmation has one action on either side. Booking adds a second trailing control and the brand shifts accordingly. B1 makes the leading brand badge, title and subtitle one cluster and pairs a white filter circle with a ringed portrait at the trailing edge. The mobile white header circles are typically 44 px, with a compact 12–16 px icon and the low shadow.

**Floating navigation.** B1 uses a 60 px white capsule with 6 px padding, 12 px side and bottom gutters and the floating shadow. The active destination is a 48 px ink pill with inline icon and 13/19.5 bold label. Other destinations stack about 15 px glyphs over compact labels. Keep the selected destination larger but reserve enough width for adjacent labels. The 56 px ink FAB sits 20 px above the nav's top and aligns 16 px from the right. Keep navigation and the creation action distinct in position and accessible naming. [Calendar viewport](references/br10-calendar-viewport.png)

## Buttons and selection

**Primary commit.** Use an ink pill with a left-set 15–16 px semibold label and a 44 px white circle at the far end for an arrow or action icon. P1 is 60 px tall with 28 px left and 8 px right padding. P3/P4 are 58 px tall with 24 px left padding. B1 detail uses a 56 px bar with 24 px left and 6 px right inset. Use this asymmetry for a clear next step. In a dark dock, invert the nesting so a white 48 px action pill with an ink circle remains clearly actionable.

**Secondary actions.** The confirmation's equal white row uses 48 px high pills, a 10 px inter-button gap, 13/19.5 semibold centered labels and about 12 px icons separated by 8 px. The exit is a 48 px transparent pill with a subtle 2 px outline. Local edit, back, share or overflow actions use a white, canvas or transparent circle depending on the layer. Give small context actions a quiet fill that echoes their surrounding surface.

**Compact actions.** The Add button is a 36 px ink pill with 14 px inline padding and a 6 px gap from its plus. A service row uses a 36 px white disc with ink plus when available and an ink disc with a white check when selected. The row is the target. Use icon-only treatment where the row already supplies the object's name. In a receipt, the small removal mark sits after a stable price edge. Give it a descriptive accessible name and sufficient target area in production.

**Tabs and segments.** A discrete selected item is ink with white label. In P1 the category rail sits in a white 48 px capsule with a 6 px inset. In P2 categories become separate 40 px pills in a scrolling row with 8 px gaps and a white count badge on the selected pill. In B1 the three branches share a 52 px white shell with 6 px padding and 4 px gaps. Equal-width 40 px segments have inline location marks. Inside the white date card, the one-day/week toggle uses a 40 px canvas shell and 32 px segments. In a full sheet, tabs use a 48 px canvas bed with 40 px segments. Use one state marker per exclusive group and a visible focus state alongside persistent selection.

**Horizontal filter pills.** B1 uses 36 px pills in a 40 px scroll lane. Pills use 14 px horizontal padding, 8 px gaps, 12/18 at 600 and a lighter count. An unselected status may carry a small deep dot. The active filter is dark and clear even when the status has a different identifying color. Peek the next pill where overflow needs discovery, keep the text intact, and allow scrolling by touch and keyboard. The calendar filter count is separate from the count within each filter.

**Badges, chips and stage indices.** Status uses a short label next to a 6–8 px dot or a small circle/check, usually in a 24–28 px pill. Neutral tags can use white at 70% on a pastel bed. A 28 px mobile or 32 px desktop white disc can carry a two-digit stage index when sequence matters. Give that disc and the title a shared centerline. Use a native emoji only as a short accent beside a purposeful heading or result. Badges convey availability, verification, count, state or task position.

## Identity hero and contextual stickers

The white hero contains status, identity and quick facts. P1's 330 px hero places copy on a 36 px inset and art in a 12 px inset on the right. P2's 343 px card adds an 8 px art inset at the top and 12 px internal copy gutters below. Keep identity larger than every nearby action label. Put labels above aligned values, and keep the copy short enough to leave a visible interval before the facts.

The P1 art panel measures about 408 × 306 px with 26 px rounding. P2 uses 327 × 210 px with 24 px rounding. The same 1600 × 1200 cutout-like scene uses a bottom-centered contain fit. Its lilac-to-peach gradient, a translucent white disc and sparse plus signs stay inside the art frame.

The rating sticker is a white disc, 74 px desktop or 62 px mobile before its 6° rotation. A small second line sits under the numeral. Anchor it to the upper trailing corner 16 px in on desktop and 12 px on mobile. Anchor the 52 or 44 px high availability sticker to the opposite lower corner. Its white-ish surface carries a dark icon tile and a two-line label/value stack. Both earn the stronger shadow through actual overlap with the art. Keep their content short and leave the figure's face or subject unobstructed. [Desktop booking](references/petal-booking-desktop-full.png)

## Bundles, choice lists and record lines

**Bundle and list relationship.** Bind the bundle and list to the same category pastel. The bundle has a white icon tile, a compact uppercase category or saving label, a bold title, an optional crossed-out original price and the current price next to a compact ink action. On desktop the 210 px bundle stands beside the list and anchors price/action near its bottom. On mobile it becomes a short horizontal row above the list. The category palette, icon and offered items can change together while the structure recurs. [Alternate category](references/petal-booking-mobile-nails.png)

**Choice row.** Use a 4 × 40 px deep accent rail with round ends. Set the name in 14–15 px semibold ink, with compact support copy. On desktop, reserve trailing lanes for duration, right-aligned tabular price and the disc. At 1440 a sample row is 666 × 68.5 px with 14 × 16 px padding and 16 px gaps. Mobile measured 331 × 82 px with 12 px padding and 12 px gaps. Its duration and price move under the description and the action stays centered at the right. Normal rows are transparent over the category bed. The selected row uses 80% white and a small contact shadow. Use its check as a second signal.

**Receipt line.** On white, use a related 4 px rail, name, optional duration or category and trailing amount. Desktop and confirmation rails are 36 px high. Mobile dock sheet rails are 32 px. Keep lines compact, with predictable trailing alignment. Editable versions expose a removal affordance. Confirmation lines omit inline editing and can use two strengths of the same category color to distinguish segments. A detail form can echo the rail on larger, softly inset selectable service tiles. [Booking summary sheet](references/petal-booking-mobile-summary-sheet.png)

## Avatars, rings and availability

**Primary profile.** Use a round photo inside a visibly separate progress ring. P1 has a 118 px ring box and 90 px photo. P2 uses 86 px and 64 px. The active deep lime arc has round caps and a pale track. The black percentage badge overlaps the photo/ring region near the bottom. Name, role and tags stand beside the portrait. Keep the ring and its text tied to the same metric.

**Selector row.** P1 places the row of 40 px avatars at the bottom of the specialist card with 10 px spacing. P2 uses 36 px avatars with 8 px gaps below a subtle divider. Active selection has an ink ring and a gap revealing the underlying lime. Other portraits lose some intensity. A white-tinted shuffle circle gives the fallback the same weight as a portrait. Provide names for icon or photo-only controls.

**Operational roster.** B1 compresses the same idea to 44 px ring boxes and 30 px portraits. Person, hours and occupancy sit beneath each ring. Below 40% uses lime, 40 to under 75% uses peach, and 75% or more uses ink. The category word and printed percentage anchor interpretation. The roster is aligned to its grid columns and stays visible during vertical scrolling. A page position dot and arrows indicate additional staff. Soft white at 95% and an 8 px backdrop blur separate the sticky header where content passes behind it. [Scrolled calendar](references/br10-calendar-scrolled.png)

**Dates, availability and slots.** Place the small weekday above the large day number. Petal expands the active date into a wider ink pill and uses a horizontal sequence at mobile width. Put a small, white month action above it. The chart uses 26 × 100 px desktop tracks or 22 × 90 px mobile tracks. White at 70% is the open track. Sky-deep at 80% rises from the base with a pill cap. Closed tracks are fainter and carry a dash. The chosen hour uses ink and exposes the exact open count above. Follow the chart with a divider and equal-width 40 px slot pills. Give the actual slot its own selected fill and expose that state semantically. A chart bar or host panel that extends out of its horizontal lane is a clipping issue to resolve.

B1's date strip uses seven equal 76 px day cells with 18 px rounding. A weekday, 17 px date, 24 × 6 px load track and occasional 4 px dot share a centerline. The chosen day has a dark body and an inverted load track. Week view keeps the day cells quiet and indicates today with the accent dot. A comparison matrix below gives corresponding percentages. ## Tickets, docks and sheets

**Ticket anatomy.** Start with the subject and status, then use a peach inset for time and participant. Add duration strips and line items. A 2 px ink/10 dashed rule capped by canvas-colored notches marks the transition to the total. The desktop and confirmation notches are 24 px in diameter and centered on the outer edges. The mobile booking sheet uses 20 px notches. Align every notch with the divider's centerline. Follow with a 24–34 px weight-800 total according to available area. A compact confirmation code panel can sit below the total. The source barcode is decorative and the human-readable code is the identification channel in the UI.

**Segmented meter.** A 10–12 px rail with 4 px gaps shows quantities in order, with widths tied to the underlying items. In confirmation the 60 and 25 minute segments occupy roughly 70.6% and 29.4% of the area remaining after the gap. Print total and time labels separately. The line items use the same accent order. Use the real track math for boundary labels. The source's middle time label is optically offset through a fixed padding, which can drift away from the represented transition.

**Nonmodal mobile dock.** Use a 351 px layer at the 375 px reference width, with 12 px viewport gutters. The 64 px ink dock has a 26 px radius, 16 px leading inset, white primary information and a 48 px nested action. Keep date/time secondary to amount. The summary stacks 8 px above the dock. Its white panel has 28 px corners, 16 px padding and a 40 × 4 px handle. The background remains legible and undimmed. Reserve room in the scroll tail so the dock cannot cover the end of the page.

**Month picker.** B1 opens a 550 px high sheet at the reference viewport. A centered 18/27, weight-800 month label sits between 40 px navigation circles. The seven-column grid uses 48 px high date cells with 16 px rounding and 4 px gaps. A 6 px load dot sits under each applicable date. Ink selects the day and inverts the dot to white. The bottom offers a legend and a full-width 48 px return-to-today pill. [Month picker](references/br10-calendar-month-sheet.png)

**Modal operations sheet.** B1 uses a full-width white bottom sheet with 32 px top corners, a 40 × 4 px handle, 20 px side padding and a 36 px canvas close button. The ink scrim at 30% with 2 px blur holds the page in context. The panel scrolls internally when tall. A 20 px 800-weight title leads filters or creation. The detail variant can lead with a sky conflict cluster, then status, subject, peach time grid, line items, perforated total and actions. Communicate focus, dismissal and return to the underlying record in a production implementation. The nearby field label communicates purpose. Suggested people sit below as 36 px neutral chips with white initials disks. A peach context inset contains the chosen date, increment/decrement circles and horizontally selectable staff. Two columns of 60 px service tiles continue below. ## Operational grids, status patterns and empty states

**Day grid.** Keep the time lane and resource header aligned. The measured layout is a 38 px leading inset, three roughly 98 px columns with 6 px gaps, and 72 px per hour across an 11-hour example. Use a white grid plane with low-contrast rules. Cards sit slightly inside the column and adjacent events share a stable time axis. An event uses a 12 px radius, a 3 px deep rail and 4/4/4/8 px content padding. Its usual reading order is time, person and service. Short events use a compact row. Overlaps share columns and open into a sheet with a switcher between conflicting records. If the inline name becomes unreadable, provide the time plus an explicit overlap count or a wider detail target.

**Status contrast.** B1 uses lilac for booked, sky for in progress, lime for completed and muted gray with dashed edge/strikethrough for cancelled. Off-hours use faint diagonal hatching, breaks use dots, blocked periods use white stripes over ink and free gaps use a lime-tinted fill with a dashed outline. Supply labels or recognizable glyphs and a wrap-capable legend. The current-time line is 2 px peach with a 10 px anchored dot, white keyline and printed time. It crosses the grid as data and requires a contrast-aware time label in production.

**Week matrix and tables.** The source weekly matrix puts person and photo on the left, days on the top and tabular values in uniformly spaced 40 px cells with 10 px corners. White space and shared row/column axes do the grouping. Dark can mean the high end of an ordinal scale here. Keep a textual legend and apply a visible selection outline to the current row, column or cell independently of its fill. To adapt to a generic table, keep the same structural alignment, muted headers and right-aligned numeric columns. Give status a small cell treatment and keep the white surface continuous.

**Empty state.** In B1 a calendar with all staff hidden keeps context and places an icon tile, concise reason and dark recovery pill inside its board area. The action restores the missing scope. Use one focal recovery action and keep the explanation grounded in the user's filter or state. Preserve the section title, relevant context and accessible announcement if the contents update in place. [Calendar empty state](references/br10-calendar-empty.png)

For a desktop side panel, select dialog or conventional data table in a new product, follow the roles demonstrated here. Use a plain white region for nearby decisions, local pastel for related constraints or status, and a focused dark action where the user commits. Derive dimensions from the actual information and target layout.
